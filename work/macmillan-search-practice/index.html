Overview

At MacMillan Search I was promoted from Automation Engineer to SEO Specialist. The work combined technical audits, search performance analysis, client collaboration and internal tooling across accounts that ranged from thousands to tens of thousands of URLs.

Alongside delivery, I contributed to tracking and revenue-attribution conversations—reading paid and organic acquisition together in GA4, Search Console and BI-style reporting so SEO priorities reflected what the measurement stack could actually support.

Content architecture, Webflow implementation, enterprise consolidation strategy and The B2B SEO editorial channel were part of the same tenure. Flagship outcomes have their own pages; those supporting workstreams are summarized below.

Results

  • 50+ technical SEO audits with prioritized implementation recommendations
  • Delivery across concurrent accounts including multi-site portfolios at thousand- to tens-of-thousands URL scale
  • Content architecture, Webflow CMS delivery, consolidation strategy and in-house B2B editorial ownership
  • Promotion from Automation Engineer into SEO Specialist delivery
  • Reusable internal tooling and QA processes across diverse architectures

The problem

SaaS, marketplace and enterprise clients needed technical SEO that scaled beyond one-off audits—especially where JavaScript rendering, large URL inventories and migration risk were involved.

Constraints

Agency delivery timelines, shared tooling, client communication expectations and the need for automation that other specialists could reuse.

What I owned

Technical auditing; automation development; organic growth initiatives; stakeholder reporting; QA processes; and collaboration with clients, developers and content teams after promotion into the SEO Specialist role.

Alternatives considered

Fully manual audits do not scale. Opaque black-box tools are hard to defend to developers. Waiting for headcount instead of tooling leaves quality inconsistent across accounts.

Why this approach

Pair rigorous audit practice with maintainable automation and clear implementation roadmaps—so findings become work developers can schedule.

Implementation & validation

Audits covering crawlability, rendering, indexation and performance; Python reporting against GSC; migration URL mapping and post-launch crawling; and content frameworks that connect search intent to site architecture. Concurrent enterprise and multi-location portfolios meant template-level findings mattered more than page-by-page edits—crawl budgets, shared components and stakeholder reporting had to work at thousand- to tens-of-thousands URL scale.

Outcome

Prioritized recommendations across diverse architectures, measurable SaaS organic growth, clean migration QA evidence and reusable tooling.

Working at portfolio scale

Several accounts were multi-site or multi-location properties where the interesting risk lived in shared templates, faceted paths and incomplete internal linking—not in any single URL. The useful response was the same loop documented in the audit methodology: investigate at crawl scale, prioritize what engineering can actually schedule, and report what still needs work separately from what already passed.\n\nConcrete investigations with stronger evidence live on Marketplace rendering and Enterprise migration QA. Organic growth measurement lives on B2B SaaS organic growth and SEO Rankings Report. Editorial automation with a clear throughput metric lives on Human-reviewed AI editorial automation.
